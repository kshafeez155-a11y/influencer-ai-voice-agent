Beta terms

Use TAC Voice like a conversation, not a weapon.

1. The beta service

TAC Voice lets you request a clearly disclosed, AI-generated web voice conversation associated with a demo persona or approved creator. It is not a call with the real person. Features, availability, call length, and personas may change or be paused during beta.

2. Who may use it

You must be at least 18 and legally able to accept these terms. Use only a microphone you control and do not include another person without their knowledge and consent.

3. Acceptable use

Do not use the service to harass, deceive, threaten, impersonate, invade privacy, violate intellectual-property or personality rights, solicit illegal activity, bypass safeguards, overload systems, or obtain high-risk instructions. Do not capture another person’s speech without their knowledge and consent.

4. AI output and high-stakes topics

AI responses are generated probabilistically and can be inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate. They are for general conversation and entertainment, not medical, legal, financial, emergency, or other professional advice. Verify important information with qualified humans.

5. Creator identity and content

Demo labels do not claim involvement by a real creator. Official creator participation requires authorisation. You may not extract, republish, or use synthetic voices to impersonate a person. Rights holders may use the report process.

6. Pricing and payments

Calls are currently free during beta. Prices shown on the pricing page are previews and are not offers for sale. Before paid features launch, checkout, tax, cancellation, refund, and renewal terms must be presented and accepted.

7. Availability and responsibility

The beta is provided on an as-available basis and may fail, disconnect, or lose context. To the extent permitted by law, the operator is not responsible for decisions made from AI output or indirect losses. Nothing here excludes rights or liability that cannot legally be excluded.

8. Suspension and changes

Access may be restricted for misuse, security, provider requirements, or legal compliance. These terms may change as the beta evolves; the updated date will change and material terms should be presented before continued use.

9. Questions

Use the contact form. The production terms must identify the legal operator and governing forum before commercial launch.
