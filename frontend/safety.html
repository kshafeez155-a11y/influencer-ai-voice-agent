Clear identity, real control

AI should never sneak into the call.

TAC Voice is built around explicit request, clear AI disclosure, creator permission, and an easy way out.

Safety basicsIdentityConsentBoundariesReporting

Know who—or what—is speaking

Every experience should identify itself as AI. Demo personas are labelled as demos. A real creator must be verified and approved before the product presents the profile as official.

Only start with clear consent

The participant must explicitly agree to live microphone processing and start the session themselves. Do not use TAC Voice to harass, deceive, or record someone else.

Keep high-stakes decisions human

AI responses can be wrong. Do not rely on a creator persona for medical, legal, financial, emergency, or mental-health crisis decisions. The system should not ask for passwords, OTPs, banking credentials, or government ID numbers.

Stop, report, and preserve only what helps

End the session if a call feels unsafe. Use the safety report for unwanted sessions, impersonation, or harmful responses. Share the approximate time and profile, but avoid unnecessary sensitive data.
