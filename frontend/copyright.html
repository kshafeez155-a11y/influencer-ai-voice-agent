Use the safety report for content, profile images, names, voice, or likeness used without authority.

Submit a rights notice →

What to include

  • Your name and reliable contact information.
  • The work, identity, voice, or likeness you represent.
  • The TAC Voice profile or content at issue.
  • Why the use is unauthorised and your relationship to the rights holder.
  • A good-faith statement that the information is accurate.

What happens next

The operator may temporarily restrict content while verifying a notice, ask for additional information, and contact the profile submitter. False or abusive notices may be rejected.

Not legal process

This beta form is an operational intake route and is not a substitute for any notice format required by applicable law. Obtain legal advice for formal claims.
