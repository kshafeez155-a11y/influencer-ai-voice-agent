Privacy in plain language

Your microphone opens one session—not a marketing list.

Last updated 24 August 2026

Beta policy draft: this page describes the current product implementation. It requires legal review before commercial launch.

Information we collect

  • Optional accounts: name, email, securely derived password hash, account role, session identifiers, and account dates. Creators also provide public profile details and a private AI conversation guide.
  • Creator voice cloning: after creator identity review, an authorised creator may upload a short voice sample to Cartesia to create a synthetic voice. TAC records the resulting voice identifier and a consent receipt. TAC does not retain the uploaded sample in its own database or file storage.
  • Web call: creator selected, optional caller name, signed-in account ID when applicable, session timing, duration, status, and technical error details. A phone number is not requested.
  • During a call: audio is transmitted for live speech recognition and speech generation. Spoken words are transcribed in real time so the AI can respond. The creator dashboard does not display transcripts or recordings.
  • Forms: contact details and the information you submit for support, safety, creator interest, feedback, or data requests.
  • Basic service data: technical logs needed to operate, secure, and diagnose the service.

Do not share passwords, OTPs, payment credentials, government ID numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

How we use information

We use account information to sign you in, remember your call history, enforce creator ownership, and operate creator profiles. We use call data to open the requested conversation, generate and deliver it, show creators aggregate connection activity, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot failures. Accounts remain optional for guest calls, and TAC Voice does not sell a marketing mailing list.

Service providers

The active web voice pipeline uses ElevenLabs for real-time speech recognition, Groq for language generation, and Cartesia for speech synthesis. Twilio telephony is currently paused. Each provider receives the data needed for its part of the call and handles it under its own terms. Hosting and network providers may also process technical data.

Because these providers may operate outside India, data may be processed in other countries subject to applicable transfer requirements.

Storage and retention

Accounts, opaque login sessions, creator profiles, public submissions, historical phone call requests, and web-call session metadata are stored in the product database. Login sessions expire after 30 days. Passwords are not stored in plain text. The beta does not yet run an automatic deletion schedule for account and call metadata; records remain until deleted for operations or in response to a verified request, except where security, dispute, or legal needs require limited retention.

Conversation context is used during a live call. This policy does not claim that every provider immediately deletes transient audio or transcripts; provider retention settings and contracts must be confirmed before commercial launch.

Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, or grievance review. Use the data request form. We may verify that the submitted email belongs to you before acting.

You can use web calls as a guest, sign out from your account, end audio processing with the End control, or unpublish a creator profile from Creator Backstage. If a session feels unsafe, end it and report it.

Children and changes

TAC Voice is intended for adults aged 18 or older. We may update this policy as the beta changes and will revise the date above. Material changes should be presented before the affected processing begins.

Contact

Use the contact form for privacy questions and the data request form to exercise a data right.
