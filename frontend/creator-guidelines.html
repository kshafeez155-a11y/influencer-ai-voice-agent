Creator operating rules

Permission is the product.

These guidelines set the minimum bar for creator identity, voice use, content boundaries, and takedown in the beta.

1. Authority and verification

The applicant must be the creator or an authorised representative. TAC Voice may request evidence of identity and representation before publishing an official profile.

2. Voice and likeness consent

Voice cloning, profile publication, and commercial use require specific approval. Public audio alone is not consent. Approval can define channels, duration, geography, and permitted uses.

3. Persona boundaries

Creators should define useful topics, prohibited claims, sensitive subjects, referral language, and words or endorsements the persona must not use. Paid or sponsored messaging must be disclosed.

4. Review and takedown

Creators review the profile and synthetic voice before launch. A verified rights holder can request a pause, correction, or removal through the reporting route.

5. Prohibited uses

No fraud, deceptive endorsements, impersonation, sexual content involving minors, targeted harassment, instructions for wrongdoing, or high-stakes professional claims presented as authoritative.
